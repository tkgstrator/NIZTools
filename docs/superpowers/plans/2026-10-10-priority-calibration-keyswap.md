Fake reads retain delayed replies across empty slices. The next method sends DD only after DA and explicit user-held-key action; wait for DE. Only one held batch in the initial experiment. Finish/cancel sends D9/1 once when the original transport remains valid and no output is outstanding; unlock transmission is not an acknowledgement.

An actually pending output remains owned until return; no competing command, close/reopen or automatic retry. Record the original failure, calibration phase, whether changing commands were attempted, settings backup, raw trace and separate cleanup result.
